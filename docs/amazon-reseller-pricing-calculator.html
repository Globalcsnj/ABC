Pawnshop reality check — what Amazon lets you sell used

NEW ONLY Don't bother listing used
  • Jewelry (fine & fashion) — must be new
  • Watches — new only; narrow Certified Pre-Owned exception needs 3rd-party certification
  • Beauty, Health & personal care, Grocery
  • Baby (except apparel), Toys & Games
USED OK Good fit for Amazon
  • Electronics, cameras, PC — allowed used (may be "gated" — need approval first)
  • Tools, musical instruments
  • Video games, books, movies, music
  • Most general merchandise — grade condition honestly

Amazon has no rule against pawnshops. But jewelry & watches — core pawn stock — are new-only, so keep those in-store or on eBay. Sell your allowed, fast-moving used goods here. Grade condition accurately (Like New / Very Good / Good / Acceptable) and keep proof of purchase for branded items.

Formula (% fee): price = (cost + shipping + storage) ÷ (1 − referral% − FBA% − margin%)
Formula ($ fee): price = (cost + shipping + storage + FBA$) ÷ (1 − referral% − margin%)
The per-item price is worked out from that item's own costs and margin. The $40/month plan is separate overhead — it doesn't change the price of any single item; instead the panel shows how many items you must sell to cover it, and your net monthly profit after it. Real FBA fulfillment is a flat per-item fee (~$3–5 for small items in 2026) that already covers shipping to the buyer — switch the fee to "$ / item" and enter Amazon's number for accuracy. Storage is billed monthly (~$0.87/ft³, ~$2.40 in Q4) plus an aged-inventory surcharge after 180 days — estimate a per-item figure for how long you expect it to sit. Returns aren't modeled; pad the margin on fragile or slow items. Numbers stay on this device only.
